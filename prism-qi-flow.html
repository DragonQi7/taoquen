The Prism of Qi

When white light passes whole through the prism, the Five Elements are in balance — this is health. But the five are one connected system. Lower any element and — through the nourishing cycle (Wood→Fire→Earth→Metal→Water→Wood) — it drags the others down with it: their colors dim, organ imbalances appear, and the whole light fades. Restore the balance and the light returns to white.
