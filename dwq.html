About the Program

Dragon's Way Qigong® Carries the Spirit
of Chinese Culture Forward

Chinese civilization has given the world two extraordinary gifts that have never stopped being relevant: Chinese Medicine as a healing art — addressing the whole person across body, mind, and spirit — and Chinese Martial Arts as a path of personal cultivation, demanding discipline, presence, and a sustained relationship with one's own inner power. Dragon's Way Qigong® was created at the intersection of both.

At the heart of Chinese culture is the mystery of the Dragon — not as myth, but as symbol of invisible power. The dragon moves between the visible world and the invisible world. It is unfathomable, timeless and mysterious. That is the spirit of the Dragon this program carries.

Dragon's Way Qigong® was built on one question: What kind of practice truly addresses the relationship between your body, mind, and consciousness? The answer unfolds across every level of the program — from the wisdom of how you eat, all the way to the direct, personal experience of Qi itself.

“Dragon's Way Qigong® is not just an exercise program, not a new scientific technique, not another wellness trend. This program shows people the source of life itself — that both the visible world and the invisible world are real.”

— Grand Master Lu, Dragon's Way Qigong® Founder

“The goal is to create balance. Yin and Yang must be balanced. When balance is achieved and Qi flows freely — disease cannot enter.”

— Grand Master Lu

Dragon's Way Qigong® is powerful for one fundamental reason: the program itself follows natural law. It is built on the same forces that govern all of life. The Qi, the source, the life force — these are not concepts. They are natural law. And that is what Dragon's Way Qigong® carries forward.

Through understanding Qi, practicing correct movements with repeated effort, and following the guidance of a lineage thousands of years in the making — the student gains real, lasting health benefits. Not theory. Not temporary relief. A genuine shift in how the body functions, how energy flows, and how life is experienced.

The Programme Arc

Six weeks.
One complete journey through the body.

Dragon's Way Qigong® does not treat symptoms in isolation. It moves through the organ systems in a deliberate sequence — the same sequence that Traditional Chinese Medicine has followed for thousands of years. Each week builds on the last.

Week 01
Foundation
Everything Is Energy

Before the programme can work, the body needs to stop spending more than it earns. Week 1 introduces the concept of Qi — your life force — and begins the process of reducing energy leaks. The Qigong practice starts with four foundational movements. The eating-for-healing plan removes the heaviest burdens on the digestive system.

“Learning to save your energy — and acquire more — is the key to your success with Dragon’s Way Qigong.”

Week 木
Liver & Gallbladder · Wood Element
The Programme Begins Here — and for Good Reason

The Liver is the focal point of the entire Dragon’s Way programme. It governs the smooth flow of Qi and blood throughout the entire body. When Liver Qi stagnates — through stress, anger, or a hectic lifestyle — every other system is affected. A healed Liver is not just one organ healed. It is the whole body’s flow restored.

“Do you want to be like the cat that is strong, agile and flexible, or like the cow which has huge muscles but little strength?”

Week 水
Kidney & Bladder · Water Element
Your Energy Tank

The Kidney stores the Qi you were born with — your constitutional reserve. It governs longevity, bone density, hormonal health, memory, and deep vitality. Most people living with chronic fatigue are quietly drawing from this reserve without replenishing it.

“Think of your life as a candle. Some candles are small, some are large. How wisely you burn your flame will determine how well you live your life.”

Week 土
Spleen & Stomach · Earth Element
Your Daily Energy Generator

The Spleen and Stomach transform food, emotion, and experience into usable energy. When this system is weak, the body draws from its Kidney reserves to compensate. Worry and overthinking are the specific emotions that deplete it fastest.

“Emotions have a stronger effect than food on our bodies. On one level, you are what you eat; at a higher level, you are what you think.”

Week 金
Lung & Large Intestine · Metal Element
Let It Go

The Lung is the great distributor of Qi. It also governs the skin, the outer immune layer, and the body’s capacity for release. Together with the Large Intestine, this pair processes not only breath and waste, but grief, sadness, and anything the body is holding onto unnecessarily.

“Every day is an opportunity to discover yourself. Celebrate the process even more than the goal. And then, let it go.”

Week 火
Heart & Small Intestine · Fire Element
The King of All Organs

The Heart is reached last — and deliberately. In TCM, the Heart is the sovereign: it governs consciousness, spirit (Shen), and the emotional life of every organ. When all other organs have been brought into balance, the Heart can finally rest in its proper place. This is where the change becomes permanent.

“If you want to heal someone, you have to first heal yourself. You can only give someone what you have.”

About the Founder

Grand Master Nan Lu —
A Living Tradition

Founder, Dragon’s Way Qigong® & Wu Ming Qigong  ·  TCM World Foundation

Grand Master Nan Lu is a living Taoist master, healer, and teacher — and one of the most rare and authentic voices in traditional Chinese healing alive today. He is the founder of Dragon’s Way Qigong® and the carrier of the Wu Ming Qigong lineage in the West. In a world full of wellness programs and self-proclaimed masters, Grand Master Lu is, simply put, the real deal.

His lineage is not a modern synthesis or a curated wellness approach. Grand Master Lu himself teaches that there are three kinds of Qigong systems in the world — and Wu Ming Qigong belongs to the first: the traditional system, transmitted master to student, generation to generation, traceable all the way back through the lineages. You can follow the chain. It does not begin with him. It runs through him — from a living line that connects back to the ancient Taoist philosophers Lao Tzu and Zhuangzi.

“The first qigong system is a traditional qigong system — a master passes to a student, the student passes to their student, generation to generation. You can trace back to their lineages. Our qigong, Wu Ming Qigong — the lineage is linked with Lao Tzu and Zhuang Tzu.”

— Grand Master Nan Lu

Grand Master Lu founded TCM World Foundation in New York with one purpose: to bring not just the techniques, but the consciousness of Traditional Chinese Medicine to the Western world. He has authored multiple books on Dragon’s Way Qigong and trained hundreds of certified instructors who carry these teachings to students across the United States and beyond.

What distinguishes Grand Master Lu from nearly any other teacher in Qigong or integrative wellness is this: he does not teach techniques alone — he encourages each student to grow. Every class, every program, every conversation he offers is designed to awaken the direct, lived experience of Qi — that invisible yet undeniable life force that ancient Chinese masters understood as the foundation of all health, harmony, and transformation.

When you study Dragon’s Way Qigong®, you are not studying a wellness trend. You are receiving something that has been preserved, passed down, and kept alive across thousands of years — for exactly this moment.
