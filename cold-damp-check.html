Cold & Dampness

Master Lu names Cold and Dampness as the two great enemies of healthy metabolism — invisible energies that slip in when your Qi is weak and bog down the Spleen & Stomach. Cold makes things slow, ache and “stick together”; Dampness makes things heavy, swollen and sluggish. Check the signs you live with, and see which may have crept in — with the book's own ways to clear it.

❄️Internal ColdNone
Slows, congeals, aches — worse in winter and before rain or snow.

❄️ Warm the Cold
“Heat it up and ice becomes water, which can flow.”
  • Ginger tea — fresh ginger root slices + a little brown sugar; sip several times a day (it goes to the Stomach).
  • Daily hot baths or hot showers to drive Cold out.
  • Protect your legs — major Spleen & Stomach meridians run up them; never bare legs in cold wind.
  • Eat warm, cooked food; cut ice cream, iced drinks and raw/cold foods.
  • Real rest, wrapped up warm — let the body catch its breath.
💧 Drain the Dampness
Damp Qi must be transformed to water, then released — diuretics only move the water, not the Damp.
  • Support the Kidney & Bladder — they transform Damp Qi into water the body can pass out.
  • Keep the Spleen & Stomach warm & dry — they love warmth and hate Damp.
  • Avoid cold-raw, greasy and over-sweet foods and damp environments that breed Damp.
  • Move and gently sweat daily — releasing Damp is a slow, steady process.
  • Build metabolism with Qigong so Damp has no place to settle.
“As long as the organs work in harmony and Qi flows freely, there is no place for disease.” — Nei Jing
