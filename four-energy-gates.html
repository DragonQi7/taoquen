Why technique matters

Knowing where a gate sits is not the same as knowing how to open it. Pressure, direction, rhythm, and the quality of your attention decide whether Qi actually moves — or whether you’re simply touching a point.

That is the difference between a diagram and a practice — and it is what the Four Energy Gates program teaches in full.
